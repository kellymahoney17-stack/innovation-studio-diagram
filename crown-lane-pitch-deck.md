**We didn't research this market. We lived in it.**

For fifteen years, this team has sat inside the financial lives of some of the world's most visible wealth creators — as their advisors, their dealmakers and their confidants. We watched the industry's most valuable clients become its most underserved.

## Slide 3 — The thesis

**Crown Lane is the private club for modern wealth.**

*No firm has ever made wealth management feel like somewhere you belong.*

Crown Lane changes that: a new type of multi-family office combining institutional services — investment management, strategic advisory, wealth structuring, personal operations — with modern technology and the community, access, and belonging the world's great private clubs figured out long ago.

It is the financial home the next generation of wealth creators has never had. Most wealth managers treat clients as portfolios. Crown Lane treats them as members.

Our vision: the first true luxury brand in financial services. Membership by invitation only.

## Slide 5 — Why now

**Three forces are converging.**

**I. A generational wealth transfer unlike anything in history**
$84 trillion is moving to a generation that does not trust legacy institutions, does not see itself in the brands those institutions built, and is actively looking for something different.

**II. AI-native infrastructure makes this economically viable for the first time**
For decades, the family office experience required hundreds of millions to build. That infrastructure now exists as best-in-class components. The firms being built today on modern stacks will deliver a client experience legacy players cannot replicate.

**III. A cultural moment where wealth is visible and identity-linked**
Athletes, founders, creators, and executives are building wealth in public. Their financial life is part of their identity. They want a firm that reflects their ambitions, their taste, and their standard, not one that assigns them an account number.

*The world's great clubs run multi-year waitlists. Belonging is the scarcest luxury.*

## Slide 6 — The member reality

**The best clients. The worst served.**

*A composite, drawn from fifteen years inside the financial lives of athletes, founders, and creators.*

**The pattern we saw, over and over**
- Nine advisors — the private bank, the agent, the business manager, the lawyer, the accountants — and no one in charge. Most have never been in the same room.
- Deals and endorsements arrive through the entourage and close without diligence.
- The capital sits in a model portfolio built for ten thousand other clients. Reporting is a quarterly PDF nobody reads.

**What Crown Lane replaces it with**
- One senior partner accountable for everything: investments, tax, structure, operations, the foundation.
- Every deal — sourced by member, the collective, or Crown Lane team alike — diligenced with institutional rigor.
- A portfolio built for each family — with access most private clients never see — and a complete, current and comprehensive picture of the balance sheet.

*There are tens of thousands of these clients. Crown Lane will serve no more than 250 of them.*

## Slide 11 — Three promises

**Everything Crown Lane builds — the platform, the partners, the houses — exists to keep three promises to every member.**

**I. Your capital works harder.**
Conflict-free investment management with an institutional CIO. True, differentiated alternatives access. Proactive strategic advisory. Every deal, JV, and endorsement diligenced with institutional rigor.

**II. Your life runs itself.**
Advanced wealth structuring, tax and estate architecture, and a COO/CFO command center that coordinates every professional in your life. Simplifying complexity and returning time to what matters.

**III. You belong somewhere.**
A capped community of peers. Offices in New York and LA that feel like homes, not floors in a tower. Signature experiences, proprietary deal flow, and relationships that compound for decades.

**Scarcity is the strategy.**

A founding group of 10 to 15. Limited annual additions. Membership capped at 250.

*Competitors can copy a product. No one can copy a community.*
